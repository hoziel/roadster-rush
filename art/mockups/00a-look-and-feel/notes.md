# Look and feel directions

The three gameplay frames use the same Austin road scene and original Roadster Rush car design. The car borrows broad electric sports-roadster cues—low planted stance, long smooth hood, compact two-seat glass cockpit, flowing rear haunches, and aerodynamic wheels—without copying a Tesla body, badge, logo, or exact detail. `A-car.png`, `B-car.png`, and `C-car.png` each show the matching model from front, front three-quarter, side, and rear views in that order.

The launch research included Tesla's October 15, 2026 Roadster event and the later POV clip of its mid-air kickflip. That stunt informed the hover/flip idea as playful game action; it is not part of the car's body design. No launch images were used or traced. Sources: [Tesla Roadster page](https://www.tesla.com/roadster), [Road & Track launch coverage](https://www.roadandtrack.com/news/a74159440/tesla-roadster-flip-float-launch-control-0-60-mph-videos/), and [Teslarati POV clip report](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-shows-off-roadster-kickflip-new-pov-video/).

## A — Toon low-poly sunset

Strengths: the crisp facets and warm horizon support quick recognition at speed; the red and cream car reads clearly against the violet asphalt. Risks: too many hard facets or bright strings can compete with coins and cones. Simplify for 3D: use one broad hood plane, two rounded fender volumes, a single cockpit/glass shell, simple wheel discs, and a few large Capitol tiers; keep the current low-poly outline and omit fine grille detail. Palette: sky `#2a1650`, `#b0407a`, horizon `#ff9a3c`; road `#4a4458`, paint `#f4e9d8`; ground `#c9a26b`, `#b88f5a`; car `#e8344e`, cream accent `#f4e9d8`; coins `#ffd23f`; cones `#ff6a1a`; glass `#2b2f4a`; tires `#1b1b22`.

## B — Soft toy / clay

Strengths: rounded pastel forms feel gentle and tactile, with the same clear road layout and car silhouette. Risks: the muted road and low-contrast props can blend together on a small screen; preserve saturated coin and cone accents. Simplify for 3D: use smooth rounded primitives, broad tire rings, one-piece roof glass, and soft ambient shading rather than small seams or surface texture. Palette: sky `#8275bd`, `#d6a7c4`, horizon `#f2c99a`; road `#817988`; paint `#f6eee2`; ground `#d9c29d`; car `#e99aa6`, cream accent `#f5e3cb`; coins `#ffe08a`; cones `#f49a67`; glass `#7a7182`; tires `#434052`.

## C — Neon dusk

Strengths: magenta and cyan rims make the car and power-up pop, while gold coins and orange cones stay readable against the dark road. Risks: glow can bloom together and obscure lane edges; keep emissive accents narrow and reserve the brightest glow for gameplay targets. Simplify for 3D: use flat dark body colors with two thin emissive strips, simple wheel faces, and no animated neon geometry on scenery. Palette: sky `#160e35`, `#392060`, horizon `#9b42a8`; road `#29243c`, paint `#b9b0ce`; ground `#4a3760`; car `#e8344e`, cream accent `#f4e9d8`; coins `#ffd23f`; cones `#ff6a1a`; glass `#2b2f4a`; tires `#1b1b22`; neon `#ff4fd8`, `#35e0ff`.

Updated the roadster concept after launch research: strengthened the broad fastback/targa sports-car read and added a hover-flip as a gameplay flourish. Kept all three styles on the same scene, retained the original car design, and excluded Tesla marks and copied launch imagery. The four-view sheets were added for modeler reference.
