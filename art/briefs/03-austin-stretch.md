# Brief 03: Austin road stretch + skyline (issue #16)

Read `00-style-guide.md` first.

**What:** the "postcard": one in-game frame (portrait 9:19.5, gameplay camera) that defines the whole look. The 3-lane road runs toward a stylized **Texas Capitol dome** on the horizon, at sunset.

**In the frame:**
- 3 lanes with cream lane markings, the player car (use the 01 mockup) at the bottom third
- Roadside: low-poly buildings, a food truck, cacti, a scooter, string lights between poles
- Skyline silhouettes against the sunset: the Capitol dome (center), the **Frost Bank tower** (owl-like top), **the Austonian** (tall, slim), and a hint of the **360 Bridge** arch on one side
- Coins and a couple of cones on the road, to check gameplay readability
- Fog fading the far road into the orange horizon

**Also show:** the same frame at **night** (neon on, string lights glowing, sky deep purple, bats near the horizon).

**Readability check:** cones and coins must pop against the road in both versions.

**Deliver:** `art/mockups/03-austin-stretch/` with a day frame, a night frame, and a sheet of the skyline silhouettes on their own.
