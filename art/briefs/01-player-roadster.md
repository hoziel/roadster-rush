# Brief 01: player roadster + paint variants (issue #14)

Read `00-style-guide.md` first.

**What:** the hero car the kid drives. An original, sleek electric two-seat roadster with a low nose, a short tail, an open top (seats visible), big friendly wheels and a slight cartoon squash. No grille (it's electric). It should feel fast and cute at the same time.

**Must show:**
- Front, side, back and 3/4 views in the default red `#e8344e`
- The gameplay camera view (behind and above, portrait), with the car taking up about a third of the screen width
- 4 paint variants on the 3/4 view: metallic blue, lime neon, white with a racing stripe, purple and pink split
- A seat with room for a simple cartoon driver head (the drivers come later)
- A subtle glow strip on the back (doubles as the brake light)

**Avoid:** Tesla badges or proportions that copy a real car, chrome detail, more than 6k triangles of complexity.

**Deliver:** `art/mockups/01-player-roadster/` with PNGs and `notes.md` (colors used, anything you'd simplify).
