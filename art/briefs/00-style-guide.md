# Roadster Rush: art style guide (for Codex)

Read this before every mockup brief. Mockups go in `art/mockups/<brief-number>-<name>/`, as PNGs plus a short `notes.md`.

## Who it's for
A young kid playing on a phone in portrait. Everything must read instantly at small size and at speed.

## Look
- **Stylized low-poly, toon or flat shading.** Chunky, rounded proportions, slightly toy-like. No realism, no fine detail, no text on models.
- **Sunset Austin.** Warm light from low on the horizon, long soft shadows, purple sky overhead going to orange at the horizon.
- **Readable shapes:** silhouettes first. If it isn't recognizable as a black silhouette, simplify it.
- **Mood:** happy, sunny, playful. Never scary. Police are friendly.

## Palette (one shared palette for every model)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Sky top | `#2a1650` |
| Sky middle | `#b0407a` |
| Horizon / fog | `#ff9a3c` / `#e9784a` |
| Road asphalt | `#4a4458` |
| Road paint | `#f4e9d8` |
| Ground / dirt | `#c9a26b`, `#b88f5a` |
| Player paint (default) | `#e8344e` |
| Coins | `#ffd23f` |
| Hazard orange (cones) | `#ff6a1a` |
| Glass / tyres | `#2b2f4a` / `#1b1b22` |
| Neon accents (night) | `#ff4fd8`, `#35e0ff` |

New colors are allowed, but list them in `notes.md` so they can join the palette texture.

## Technical limits (so it can become a real model)
- Low-poly: the player car is about 3–6k triangles, a prop under 1k, a building under 2k.
- No textures beyond the flat palette (no photo textures, no decals with text).
- Show **front, side, back and 3/4 views** for 3D assets, on a neutral background, plus one in-game shot from the gameplay camera (behind and above the car, portrait 9:19.5).

## Legal (the repo goes public)
- **No real brands:** no Tesla logo, T badge, wordmark or exact Tesla body shape. The roadster is an *original* sleek electric two-seater.
- No copying another game's characters or art (for example Subway Surfers).
- Landmarks are stylized silhouettes, not copies of copyrighted renders.
