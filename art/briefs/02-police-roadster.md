# Brief 02: friendly police roadster (issue #15)

Read `00-style-guide.md` and look at the approved `01` mockup first: **same body** as the player car.

**What:** the friendly police car that follows the player. It uses the same roadster body with police paint (white and navy, or white and sky blue), a chunky toy-like light bar (red and blue), a star emblem (no text, no real department marks) and a smiling front.

**Must show:**
- 3/4, front and back views
- The light bar both off and flashing
- A gameplay shot: the police car small in the distance behind the player, seen from the gameplay camera

**Tone:** a playful chase, never threatening. Think toy police car.
