Create ONE exceptionally polished opening/title screen for the original mobile game ROADSTER RUSH: AUSTIN. This is a finished art-direction mockup, not a wireframe. Tall portrait 9:19.5 canvas (ideally 1152 x 2496). Full-bleed game screen, no device bezel, no outside presentation labels.

Do not redesign it as a generic convertible, and no brand logos. Reference image 2 gives its rear silhouette only; reference image 3 is the friendly police variant, optional as a tiny background supporting car, not the main hero. These are model references, not backgrounds to copy.
Art direction: premium stylized low-poly 3D toy world with crisp designed facets, beautifully controlled satin materials, soft ambient occlusion, warm rim light, considered composition, subtle bloom only on light sources. Warm sunset Austin: violet #2a1650 above, magenta #b0407a into apricot #ff9a3c at horizon. Red car #e8344e, cream #f4e9d8, gold #ffd23f. No photorealism or noisy textures.
Composition: upper 30 percent features spectacular but very readable bespoke game title typography with confident chunky rounded letterforms, cream face and plum dimensional depth. Exact text stacked: "ROADSTER" then "RUSH", with small "AUSTIN" underneath. Under it, stylized Capitol dome centered at distant horizon, owl-top and slim skyline silhouettes, warm terracotta low-rise shops and a little greenery. A delightful sweeping road and curved curb form a stage for the large red roadster, front three-quarter perspective, filling the middle 40 percent. Keep its silhouette recognizable from supplied model. A few floating gold coins and restrained soft dust/air create energy, but leave UI calm.
Lower 25 percent: one generously sized wide amber 3D button, perfectly aligned and readable: "LET'S DRIVE" with a small play triangle. Beneath, two smaller cream/plum rounded icon buttons labeled "GARAGE" and "DRIVERS". Tiny tasteful bottom row "KID MODE" with an enabled green toggle. No other text. No monetization, promotions, ads, badges, currencies or login.
High craft: deliberate spacing, cohesive UI material system, beautiful smooth sky gradient, layered atmospheric depth, large tap targets, safe margins. Joyful and confident, never aggressive. No weapons, real company marks, department insignia, trademarks, watermarks or copied game art.
