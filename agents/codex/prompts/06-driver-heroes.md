# Driver hero cutouts — Revision 1

Built-in imagegen, 2026-11-10. No CLI/API fallback. Input characters are Ofir-supplied illustrated reference files already in the repository. Only technical resizing/PNG compression applied afterward; genuine transparency retained.

## Nevo

References: `assets-src/drivers/nevo-full.jpg`, `art/mockups/01-player-roadster/doors-open.png`.

```text
Use case: character-and-product cutout for a game.
Make ONE polished landscape hero asset on a truly TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND, composition approximately 9:7 (target export 720 by 560). Reference 1 is the EXACT character identity to preserve: same face, facial proportions, hair, expression, outfit design and age. Reference 2 is our original roadster geometry: preserve this body design and its butterfly door mechanism, translating the flat model into the polished illustrated game style. The car faces toward lower right in front three-quarter view. Only the near-side butterfly door is raised; far door closed. Full car and all wheels visible, not cropped.
Place the full-body character standing on the LEFT beside the open doorway, clearly separate from the car silhouette, feet fully visible, one hand on hip and the other raised in a friendly wave. Keep their face unobscured by the raised door. Character height about 72% of canvas; car spans about 70% of width, with the car nose toward right edge. Both grounded on a common baseline with only a subtle soft contact shadow on transparency. Leave 5% transparent margin around the full group. The cutout must read cleanly at 360 px width.
Clean hand-illustrated character matching the reference, crisp contours and warm sunset rim lighting; polished stylized low-poly/toy roadster with dark canopy and silver spoked wheels, soft highlights, no photo texture. NO environment, floor plane, backdrop, UI, decorative frame, words, logos, sponsor patches, badges, name labels or watermark. Remove all reference suit lettering/brands; retain plain colored fabric panels and neutral seams. Preserve genuine alpha transparency, not a drawn checkerboard. Keep anatomy natural and age consistent.
Character: NEVO, the boy in reference 1. Use the CURRENT dark charcoal factory coveralls with red shoulder/side accents, spiky brown hair and clear safety goggles resting above his eyes. Preserve his brown eyes and friendly face. Remove Tesla logos, all name badges and other text. His roadster is soft WHITE #f3f1ec. No cap, no blue/orange racing suit. The output is Nevo and the white roadster together as one cutout.
```

Generated images are saved to ~/.codex/generated_images/01a1c395-d433-7ba3-a364-b7cac5d9afaa as ~/.codex/generated_images/01a1c395-d433-7ba3-a364-b7cac5d9afaa/exec-84d1b7a8-d70c-43d4-a807-84fd930505b8.png by default.
