## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single flat albedo texture for the side of a toy roadster in a browser game, not an image of a car. Create an original, exquisitely painted watercolour cherry-blossom composition on an opaque cool pearl-white ground. Wide 2:1 landscape canvas, ideally 2048 by 1024. The whole canvas IS the texture: no borders, labels, text, mockup, perspective, cast shadows or photographed paper.

Layout is essential: horizontal coordinate 0 at the left is the car nose; right is the tail. Soft cool pink-lilac blush at the left edge fades to nearly white by x=35%. Keep the rightmost quarter mostly pure pearl-white, with only 3–5 drifting pale pink petals. Keep the top 10% and bottom 10% completely free of branches, flowers or speckles, with only the quiet base wash; these strips stretch across the car's top and sills. Most of the entire texture must remain white.

One elegant dark plum branch starts low at x=32%, y=83%, tapers and sweeps toward x=68%, y=35%, with a few fine organic twigs and tiny pink buds. Concentrate the flowers across the middle third of the canvas, between x=35% and x=66%, and y=24% to y=80%. Three large hero blossoms and two smaller supporting blossoms, loosely spaced rather than a dense bouquet. Each hero bloom about 15–18% of canvas width, visually distinct, simple five-petal silhouettes with softly notched rounded petals, deep berry-pink hearts, delicate dark stamens, pale cool pink and lilac edges, layered translucent-looking pigment washes (but the final image is fully opaque), subtle capillary pigment pooling and gentle brush variation. Fine branch brushstrokes and restrained buds. Lovely, playful, feminine, airy and polished; a hand-painted premium watercolour wrap. Large flowers must stay recognizable when shrunk to a small mobile-game car. Do not make a repeated wallpaper, botanical plate, rose, dense floral carpet, or salmon/orange painting. No logos, words, signatures or watermark. No leaves needed. No real wrap copied.
