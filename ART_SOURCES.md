Rules: the repo goes public with full history, so only CC0, CC-BY (credited) and OFL/MIT are allowed. CC-BY-SA is avoided; NC, ND, Sketchfab Standard, Pixabay and store assets are out. "Contents" notes for Kenney kits come from preview images, so confirm them when downloading.

## Recommended kit family
**Kenney is the base.** Car Kit, City Kit Commercial/Suburban/Roads, Nature Kit and UI Pack share one scale, a flat-color style and GLB exports, so they mix without adjustment. **Quaternius** (Bull/Cow, Bat, Sushi Truck, cacti) is the second family. It uses the same flat, untextured low-poly style and mixes well with Kenney once both are remapped to the shared palette texture. Expect a scale normalization pass, since Quaternius models are bigger. Use one-off Sketchfab CC-BY models only for the Capitol (and maybe the scooter) and restyle them to match. Every CC-BY item goes into `CREDITS.md` and the Credits screen: bartdenny (Capitol), MiniPoly (scooter), Zafflex (if that longhorn is used).
